# NovaCart Refund and Return Policy
**Policy ID:** NC-RR-101 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 15 January 2026

## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy governs return and refund recommendations for synthetic NovaCart consumer orders. A recommendation is not a completed financial action.

## 2. Standard 30-day window
Delivered items may generally be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. The item must not show intentional damage, misuse, or material alteration. Agents must verify delivery date, order status, and return condition before recommending a refund.

## 3. Undelivered and in-transit orders
Orders that have not been delivered are not eligible for a normal post-delivery refund. In-transit and delayed orders require a delivery update or shipment investigation. A carrier loss determination or approved exception is required before any refund draft.

## 4. High-value and exception controls
Any refund above ₹10,000 requires manager approval. Requests outside the 30-day window, conflicting records, and policy exceptions also require manager approval. The copilot may prepare a draft only; it never executes refunds.

## 5. Returned items and payment status
When an item is already returned, the agent should report the recorded return/refund status and avoid duplicate refund recommendations. Payment and return records must be reconciled before escalation.

## 6. Examples
- A ₹2,499 delivered item requested on day 12 may be conditionally eligible after condition verification.
- A ₹12,999 smartwatch on day 8 requires manager approval even when otherwise eligible.
- A delivered item requested on day 45 is not automatically eligible; an exception must be reviewed.
- An in-transit parcel receives a shipment update or investigation, not a post-delivery refund.
