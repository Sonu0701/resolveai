# NovaCart Damaged Product and Replacement Policy
**Policy ID:** NC-DP-204 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 15 January 2026

## 1. Eligibility
A damaged or defective item reported within 30 calendar days of delivery is generally eligible for a replacement recommendation after verification. Delivery date and order identity must be confirmed.

## 2. Evidence requirements
Request clear photos of the product, packaging, and shipping label where appropriate. Evidence helps distinguish transit damage, manufacturing defect, and misuse. Never request sensitive identity documents in a normal damage claim.

## 3. Replacement and refund order
When replacement stock is available, replacement is the preferred recommendation. If replacement stock is unavailable, a refund may be considered under the Refund and Return Policy. No action is completed until an authorised operator performs it outside this demo.

## 4. High-value and misuse controls
Claims above ₹10,000 require manager approval. Suspected intentional damage or misuse blocks automatic resolution and requires human review. Conflicting order data also requires review.

## 5. Examples
- Headphones worth ₹3,499 damaged on day 2 with stock available: recommend replacement, pending photo evidence.
- A ₹14,999 watch damaged on day 5: recommend the eligible path but require manager approval.
- A damaged item reported on day 41: do not auto-approve; escalate for exception review.
