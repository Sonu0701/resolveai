# NovaCart Wrong Item Policy
**Policy ID:** NC-WI-415 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 15 January 2026

## 1. Claim verification
Verify the order ID, ordered SKU or product name, and the item actually received. Ask for a photo of the received item and shipping label. Do not collect unrelated personal documents.

## 2. Standard resolution
Wrong-item claims reported within 30 days of delivery are generally eligible for a return-and-replacement recommendation after verification. If correct replacement stock is unavailable, a refund may be considered under the Refund and Return Policy.

## 3. Controls
Orders above ₹10,000, repeated disputes, conflicting warehouse records, and claims outside 30 days require manager review. Historical tickets are guidance only and cannot replace this policy.

## 4. Examples
- Customer ordered black headphones but received a blue speaker on day 4: verify photos, then recommend replacement.
- A ₹18,000 wrong-item claim: verify evidence and obtain manager approval.
