# NovaCart Shipping and Delivery Policy
**Policy ID:** NC-SD-310 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 15 January 2026

## 1. Tracking states
`in_transit` means the carrier still controls the parcel. `delayed` means the expected date has passed or a carrier exception exists. `delivered` is a carrier scan and is not by itself proof the customer personally received the parcel.

## 2. Delay handling
Provide the latest tracking fact for short delays. Open a shipment investigation when the parcel is marked delayed, lacks movement, or is seven or more days beyond the expected date. Extended delay, repeated contact, or conflicting scans may require human escalation.

## 3. Delivered but not received
A delivered-but-not-received claim requires carrier investigation. Verify the delivery address, safe-place/household receipt, and delivery scan where appropriate. High-value cases above ₹10,000 require human review.

## 4. Refund boundaries
In-transit orders cannot be automatically refunded as post-delivery returns. A refund may be considered only after carrier loss confirmation or an approved exception. The support response must not claim a financial action was completed.

## 5. Examples
- Two days late with active movement: provide an update and monitor.
- Twelve days late with no movement: recommend shipment investigation.
- Marked delivered but not received: verify address and start carrier investigation.
